Privacy

In short: I collect as little as possible

This site is a tool, not a shop. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, and no data passed to third parties for marketing. Whatever does get processed is listed here in full.

No cookies from me

This site sets no cookies and writes nothing to your browser's localStorage or sessionStorage. That is also why you see no cookie banner: there is nothing for you to consent to.

The configurator runs entirely in your browser. The lantern you build never leaves your device — the STL file is generated locally and downloaded locally.

Reach measurement without personal data

I want to know whether anyone reads this. For that, the server counts two things: a page view and a download. A page view carries the page (e.g. /configurator.html), the language, the device class (desktop, phone, tablet) and — if present — the page you came from.

To count, the server builds a hash from your IP address, your browser identifier and the calendar day, and stores only that hash. The IP address cannot be reconstructed from it. Daily and hourly counters are aggregated after a short time, and old entries are deleted within 400 days at the latest.

This is not a consent matter under § 25 TDDDG, because nothing is stored on or read from your device, and it is not profiling. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in knowing whether the site is read at all (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — you may object under Art. 21.

If you use the contact form

Then I receive what you enter: name, email address, topic, an optional project description and your message. That data lives in a database at Supabase (Frankfurt am Main region, EU) and is used only to reply to you.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (responding to your enquiry) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering). To keep spam out, I also store a daily hash of your IP address — not the IP itself. I delete messages once they are settled, and at the latest after two years.

Your message does not go through a third-party form service; it lands directly with me. There is no onward transfer, unless I am legally obliged to do so.

Who is technically involved

GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA) serves the pages. The host keeps server logs with IP addresses that I cannot access. This is covered by GitHub's standard contractual clauses.

Supabase (Supabase Inc., database inside the EU) stores the counters and the contact messages. No fonts, no scripts, no tracking pixels from Google or any ad network — the fonts are hosted locally on the same server.

Your rights

You may at any time request access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20), and you may object to processing (Art. 21). A plain email is enough.

You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — the competent one is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg, Germany.
